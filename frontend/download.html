📲 Installation Instructions

  1. Tap Download Android APK above.
  2. Open Downloads or pull down your phone notification tray.

  4. Allow "Install unknown apps" in your browser settings if prompted.
  5. Tap Install, then launch SAARTH!

🌐 Already using SAARTH on Web?

You can also install SAARTH as a Progressive Web App (PWA):

  • Android Chrome: Open saarthh.in, tap menu (⋮) → "Install App" or "Add to Home screen".
  • iPhone Safari: Open saarthh.in in Safari, tap the Share button (square with arrow) → "Add to Home Screen".
